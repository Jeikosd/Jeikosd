### Hello, I'm Jeison 👋

I am a statistician and an AI engineer at [Globant](https://www.globant.com/), where I work on the agent design of LLM systems. I work in R and Python, and I like data that moves through time. My name sounds like "Jason".

I studied statistics at Universidad del Valle, with a thesis on estimating the fractional differencing parameter in long-memory time series. I then spent five years at CIAT, the International Center for Tropical Agriculture, on climate and agriculture: crop simulations and the models behind AClimate Colombia's seasonal forecasts. Since 2021 I have been at Globant, working on churn, segmentation, pricing, forecasting and recommendation, and now on LLM agents. The thread through it all is state-space models, Kalman filters and honest uncertainty.

I live in Cali, Colombia.
